title: 'PRISM: Calibrated Bayesian Fusion and Auditable Attribution for Reliable LLM
  Event Prediction from Text'
abstract: We study event prediction from news text when intermediate drivers are not
  expert-annotated. Directly prompting large language models ({LLMs}) for forecast
  probabilities can yield miscalibrated outputs and explanations that are hard to
  audit. We propose the Probabilistic Reliability and Interpretability System, PRISM,
  which separates semantic extraction from probabilistic decision making. PRISM uses
  {LLMs} only to extract interpretable factors, treats repeated extractions as noisy
  measurements of latent states, and performs {Bayesian} inference to produce posterior
  predictive probabilities that propagate extraction and measurement uncertainty.
  For empirical reliability under temporal dependence, we add low-capacity post-hoc
  calibration and time-adaptive conformal prediction and report calibration and coverage
  diagnostics. PRISM also reports factor-contrast association summaries with uncertainty
  and explicit interpretation boundaries. On a {UCDP}–{GDELT} conflict benchmark,
  PRISM achieves AUROC 0.821 and {ECE} 0.058 on the test set; at 90% target coverage,
  it attains empirical coverage 0.908 with average set size 1.38.
