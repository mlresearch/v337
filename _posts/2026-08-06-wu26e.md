title: 'DRL-ORA: Distributional Reinforcement Learning with Online Epistemic Risk
  Adaptation'
abstract: One of the main challenges in reinforcement learning ({RL}) is that the
  agent has to make decisions that would influence the future performance without
  having complete knowledge of the environment. Dynamically adjusting the level of
  epistemic risk during the learning process can help to achieve reliable policies
  in safety-critical settings with better efficiency. In this work, we propose a new
  framework, Distributional {RL} with Online Epistemic Risk Adaptation ({DRL-ORA}).
  This framework quantifies both epistemic and implicit aleatory uncertainties in
  a unified manner and dynamically adjusts the epistemic risk levels by solving a
  total variation minimization problem online. The framework generalizes the existing
  variants of risk adaptation approaches with better explainability and flexibility.
  The selection of risk levels is performed efficiently via a Follow-The-Leader-type
  algorithm. We show that {DRL-ORA} outperforms existing methods that rely on fixed
  risk levels or manually designed risk level adaptation in multiple classes of tasks.
