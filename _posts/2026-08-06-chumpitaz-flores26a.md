title: Which Directions Matter? Sparse Design for Affine Robust Optimization
abstract: Robust machine learning and optimization rely on the uncertainty model choice.
  We investigate which uncertainty directions a model must cover when defined by a
  finite dictionary and a budget constraint. Selecting a subset forms an atomic uncertainty
  set with a closed form support function, yielding tractable robust programs for
  affine objectives. We propose a data-driven selection rule based on a coverage objective
  over evaluation directions, including gradients, adversarial perturbations, or shifts
  observed on held out data. We prove this objective is monotone and submodular, supporting
  a greedy method with a $(1-1/e)$ approximation guarantee and a matching hardness
  barrier. We also provide a certificate bounding the loss from the selected subset
  and a radius calibration rule with out-of-sample control.
