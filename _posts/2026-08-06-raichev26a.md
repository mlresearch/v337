title: Variance Estimation and Selecting Good  Estimands for Causal Effect Queries
abstract: 'This paper investigates the statistical efficiency of algebraic expressions
  ("estimands") used to answer causal queries. We first examine structural rules,
  developing a partial dominance relationship for front-door estimands that extends
  recent results on back-door estimands. In many models, however, structural rules
  alone may be insufficient to select an estimand. For such cases, we propose empirical
  techniques for estimating and comparing the variance of different estimands using
  both the graph and observational data: 1) a bootstrap-based method, and 2) a computationally
  simpler yet practically effective method for discrete models which estimates the
  {Fisher} information matrices. We illustrate both methods’ effectiveness on a variety
  of causal diagrams and estimand forms.'
