title: 'Breaking Bad: Component-Wise Parent Deletion for Score-Based Causal Discovery'
abstract: Directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) are widely used to represent complex causal
  relationships in real-world systems. The goal of causal discovery is to learn the
  underlying {DAG} from data generated by these systems. While *Greedy Equivalence
  Search* (GES) is a well-established score-based algorithm for causal discovery,
  the GES family often suffers from scalability and sample complexity issues due to
  its large search space and susceptibility to local optima. In this paper, we introduce
  *parent deletion*, a novel, simple, yet powerful operator for score-based causal
  discovery. This operator is theoretically sound and effectively alleviates these
  limitations. Moreover, its simplicity enables seamless compatibility with existing
  score-based methods, and extensive experiments demonstrate consistent improvements
  across a wide range of settings. The source code used in this study is available
  at https://github.com/LGAI-Research/breaking-bad.
