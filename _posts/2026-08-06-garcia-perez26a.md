title: 'Advances in PAC-Bayesian certification of deep neural networks: tighter closed-form
  inequalities and optimization of bounds on non-differentiable losses'
abstract: This paper presents three theoretical contributions that improve the usability
  of risk certificates for neural networks based on {PAC-Bayes} bounds. First, two
  bounds on the KL divergence between {Bernoulli} distributions enable the derivation
  of the tightest explicit bounds on the true risk of classifiers across different
  ranges of empirical risk. Then, a novel method to optimize bounds on non-differentiable
  objectives is introduced, allowing to optimize risk bounds directly on the 0-1 loss
  instead of resourcing to a surrogate differentiable loss, such as the bounded cross-entropy.
  These theoretical contributions are illustrated with an empirical evaluation on
  the {MNIST} and {CIFAR-10} datasets. In fact, this paper presents the first non
  data-dependent generalization bounds on the 0-1 loss for neural networks fitted
  on {CIFAR-10}.
